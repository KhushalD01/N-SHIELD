1. Executive Summary & Problem Understanding

N-SHIELD is an AI-powered urban safety intelligence model designed to predict crime-risk areas, provide geospatial intelligence, and assist citizens, first responders, and municipal authorities in making proactive safety decisions in Nagpur, Maharashtra.

1. Reactive Policing Paradigm
  • Traditional crime prevention depends on post-incident FIR records.
  • Citizens lack predictive foresight when traveling through unfamiliar wards at night.
  • High-risk corridors (Outer Ring Road, Pardi, Kalamna) lack real-time risk indicators.
2. Golden Hour Delays
  • Trauma and cardiac patients face crucial delays reaching hospitals.
  • Generic map pins land on road centers or closed outpatient clinics.
  • No instant triage for verified 24/7 Casualty gates vs OPD clinics.
3. Distress Disconnect
  • Commuters in danger cannot comfortably make phone calls.
  • Third-party apps force guardians to create mutual accounts.
  • No continuous telemetry streaming movement speed, heading, and battery %.

3. Core Intelligence Modules

A. Dynamic Crime Risk Engine

Calculates dynamic risk (0–100) using a 4-factor stochastic formula:

Risk = [0.38·Crime + 0.27·Time + 0.22·(1-CCTV) + 0.13·(1-Lighting)] × 100
  • Time Multiplier: Escalates between 22:00 and 04:00 IST.
  • Lighting Index: Drops from 95% at noon to 8–22% past midnight.
  • Explainable AI (XAI): Breaks down exact factor percentages for every sector.
B. Safe Route Recommendation

Evaluates candidate routes using a 750-meter safety envelope:

  • Contrasts shortest travel time against surrounding incident exposure.
  • Reroutes commuters through well-lit, police-patrolled arterial roads.
  • Provides turn-by-turn safety rationale (e.g., 'Bypasses unlit canal stretch with recent snatching incidents').
C. Emergency Circle & Live Telemetry

One-tap SOS live location sharing with zero-install access:

  • Zero-Install URL: Loved ones open /live-share/[token] on any mobile browser without an account.
  • Continuous Telemetry: Streams moving GPS marker, movement velocity (km/h), compass heading, and battery %.
  • Ephemeral Privacy: Cryptographic 32-byte tokens auto-expire after 15–60 mins.
D. CCTV Intelligence & Blind Spots

Surveillance optimization for Nagpur Police and Municipal Corporation:

  • Calculates camera field-of-view (FOV) cones across major squares.
  • Identifies blind spots where high crime risk (>75) intersects with low CCTV coverage (<45%).
  • Generates pulsing crimson warning rings for prioritized police patrol scheduling.
