1. Executive Summary & Project Introduction

N-SHIELD (Nagpur Safety, Hazard Intelligence & Emergency Location Director) represents a paradigm shift from reactive law enforcement to predictive, preventative urban safety intelligence. Engineered as a full-stack digital twin for Nagpur City, Maharashtra, the platform unifies heterogeneous municipal data—including 30-day rolling FIR crime records, ambient street illumination sensor indices, Smart City CCTV camera field-of-view cones, verified hospital emergency casualty gates, and live commuter telemetry—into an actionable, sub-second decision-support ecosystem.

1.1 Urban Safety Bottlenecks in Nagpur

As Nagpur experiences exponential urban growth across outward corridors such as Wardha Road (MIHAN SEZ), Outer Ring Road, Kalamna, Pardi, and Besa-Beltarodi, safety infrastructure exhibits critical structural disparities:

  • Street Lighting Asymmetry: While core commercial avenues (West High Court Road, Central Avenue) possess modern LED lighting, transit connectors and residential interior roads experience severe illumination deficits (dropping below 20% after 22:00 IST).
  • Surveillance Blind Spots: Central intersections are densely monitored by municipal cameras, but high-traffic industrial and transit fringes (e.g., Kalamna freight market, Outer Ring Road bypasses) suffer from surveillance dead-zones where crime vulnerability escalates.
  • The Emergency Golden-Hour Dilemma: In road collisions, acute trauma, and cardiac events, family members and first responders frequently waste 15 to 25 critical minutes navigating to closed clinics or inaccurate map pins that point to the middle of roads rather than physical 24/7 casualty gates.

3. Detailed Feature Breakdown & Mathematical Formulations

3.1 Feature A: AI-Based Crime Risk Prediction Engine

Crime risk is modeled as a spatiotemporal continuum that fluctuates based on human mobility, lighting, natural surveillance, and police presence. N-SHIELD evaluates sector risk using a Multi-Factor Stochastic Formulation:

Risk Score R_z(t) = min(100, Round([ w1·L_incident + w2·F_time(t) + w3·D_cctv + w4·D_lighting(t) ] × 100))

Parameter Specifications:

  • L_incident (Incident Load, w1 = 0.38): Evaluates rolling 30-day FIR records: min(1.0, N_recent·0.08 + N_high·0.12 + I_severity·0.35), with severity weights: Critical = 1.0, High = 0.78, Medium = 0.50, Low = 0.25.
  • F_time(t) (Time Multiplier, w2 = 0.27): Non-linear escalation peaking between 22:00 and 04:00 IST (Daylight: 0.18, Dusk: 0.45, Peak Night: 0.72–1.00, Late Night: 0.62).
  • D_cctv (Surveillance Deficit, w3 = 0.22): (100 - CCTV Coverage %) / 100.
  • D_lighting(t) (Illumination Deficit, w4 = 0.13): Derived from municipal streetlight cycles, dropping from 95% at noon to 8–22% past midnight.

3.2 Feature B: Interactive Heatmap & Contiguous Municipal Polygons

Instead of imprecise circular radius overlays that create gaps and overlapping artifacts, N-SHIELD implements a 16-sector planar contiguous polygon mosaic (`src/lib/nagpurPolygons.ts`). Every sector boundary shares canonical junction vertices with its neighbors, guaranteeing zero unintentional gaps and zero visual overlap across Mankapur, Kamptee Road, Sadar, Dharampeth, Sitabuldi, Mahal, Medical Square, Wardha Road, and Kalamna.

3.3 Feature C: Safe Route Recommendation (750m Corridor Analysis)

Traditional navigation engines optimize travel strictly for shortest travel time, routing lone commuters down unlit service lanes or isolated alleys. N-SHIELD evaluates candidate paths using a 750-meter safety envelope:

  • Corridor Scanning: Evaluates street lighting percentages, active CCTV camera visibility, and recent incident clusters within 750 meters of the candidate polyline.
  • Dynamic Incident Re-Routing: If a recent disruption (protest, unlit stretch, snatching incident) is detected along the primary route, the engine recalculates an alternative corridor along illuminated, police-patrolled arterial avenues (e.g., West High Court Road).
  • Explainable Safety Advice: Supplies transparent justification: "Rerouted via WHC Road (+350m, +2 mins). Avoids unlit canal stretch with 4 recent evening snatching logs. Recommended route has 92% illumination and active PCR patrol presence."

3.4 Feature D: CCTV Intelligence & Automated Blind-Spot Discovery

A municipal coordinate is classified as an Urgent Surveillance Blind Spot when:

Blind Spot Condition = [ RiskScore(x, y, t) >= 75 ] AND [ Operational_CCTV_Density(x, y) < 45% ]

The system calculates camera field-of-view (FOV) cones across major squares (Variety Square, Zero Mile, RBI Chowk, Rahate Colony). Where high historical incidents intersect with surveillance dead-zones, the dashboard projects pulsing crimson warning rings, enabling police commanders to deploy stationary PCR vans to close surveillance voids during peak night hours.

3.5 Feature E: Nagpur Emergency Medical Directory (100+ Facilities)

N-SHIELD maintains a hand-audited directory of over 100 verified healthcare institutions across Nagpur (`src/lib/hospitalDirectoryData.ts`). Every record is audited down to the physical emergency casualty gate coordinates, eliminating road-center clustering:

  • Government Apex Centers: GMCH Medical Square (1,400 beds, Level-1 Trauma), IGGMCH Mayo Hospital (Central Avenue), AIIMS Nagpur (MIHAN SEZ).
  • Premier Multispecialty Care: Kingsway Hospital, Alexis/Max Super Speciality (Mankapur), Wockhardt Super Speciality (Shankar Nagar), Orange City Hospital.
  • Instant Triage Filters: 24/7 Verified Emergency Casualty, Government facilities, Cardiac Cath Labs, Pediatric ICUs, Maternity trauma care, and 24/7 Pharmacies with 1-tap phone dialing.

3.6 Feature F: Emergency Circle & Live Telemetry Streamer

Most SOS applications fail because they send a single static SMS coordinate, and force recipients to install heavy proprietary apps. N-SHIELD solves this through Zero-Install Ephemeral Live Sharing:

1-Tap Distress Trigger

Tapping SOS instantly creates an encrypted 32-byte session token, dispatches SMS/WhatsApp alerts with a secure web link, triggers the 112 police dialer, and begins streaming GPS coordinates every 3 to 5 seconds.

Zero-Install Recipient View

Guardians open /live-share/[token] on any mobile browser without an account. Streams real-time moving markers, travel velocity (km/h), compass heading, and device battery percentage with auto-expiry in 15–60 minutes.

3.7 Features G–J: Night Safety, Events, News & AI Assistant

  • Night Safety Mode: Dark-adapted glassmorphism UI with Web Audio API synthesized alarm frequencies (880Hz alternating warning tones).
  • Public Event Simulator: Mathematical crowd risk formula predicting safety spillovers for matches at VCA Stadium or rallies at Kasturchand Park.
  • Live Crime Intelligence: Multi-filter keyword pipeline ingesting verified local police FIR news while discarding unverified rumors.
  • AI Safety Assistant: Context-grounded Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) using Google Gemini 2.5 Flash, delivering sub-800ms natural language safety advice with mandatory 112/108 helpline guardrails.

6. Security, Privacy & Societal Impact

6.1 Location Privacy & Ephemeral Telemetry

Citizen privacy is foundational to N-SHIELD. In strict accordance with digital privacy ethics, location telemetry is treated as ephemeral:

  • Zero Persistent Movement History: The live-telemetry store records GPS coordinates exclusively during active emergency sessions. Once terminated by the user or upon reaching timeout (15–60 mins), coordinate history is permanently purged from server memory.
  • Cryptographic 32-Byte Session Tokens: URLs utilize high-entropy cryptographic strings generated via crypto.randomBytes(32), making URL guessing or enumeration computationally impossible.
  • Secure Authentication & Rate Limiting: PBKDF2/SHA-256 password hashing with unique per-user salts, HttpOnly session cookies, and sliding-window rate limiting (30 requests/min per IP) preventing automated scraping and denial-of-service attacks.

Ethical AI & Predictive Disclaimer: Algorithmic risk scores generated by N-SHIELD are decision-support indicators based on historical crime reports, municipal lighting calculations, and surveillance density. They do not constitute an absolute guarantee of safety, nor do they predict with certainty that an offence will occur. Citizens must always maintain situational awareness, obey official law enforcement advisories, and contact emergency services (112 Police / 108 Ambulance) when facing imminent danger.
