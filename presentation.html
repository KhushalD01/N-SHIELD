PROBLEM STATEMENT
Urban Safety Bottlenecks in Nagpur
1. Reactive Crime Records
  • Traditional policing relies on post-incident FIR logs.
  • Citizens lack foresight when entering unfamiliar corridors at night.
  • High-risk areas (Outer Ring Rd, Kalamna) lack live warnings.
2. Golden Hour Delays
  • Trauma and cardiac patients face crucial delays.
  • Outdated online map pins point to closed clinics or road centers.
  • No distinction between 24/7 Casualty vs OPD clinics.
3. Distress Disconnect
  • Distressed users cannot make lengthy phone calls.
  • Guardians forced to install heavy proprietary apps.
  • No streaming telemetry of battery %, speed, or heading.
Speaker Script: "At 11 PM, traditional navigation apps route commuters down unlit service lanes to save 2 minutes. When emergencies occur, hospital listings are often outdated, and distress alerts lack live telemetry."

ALGORITHMIC MODELING
Multi-Factor Dynamic Risk Scoring
The Mathematical Formulation
  • Formula: Risk = [w₁·Crime + w₂·(1-Lighting) + w₃·Time + w₄·(1-CCTV)] × 100
  • Historical Crime (w₁ = 0.35): Density of past FIR records.
  • Street Illumination (w₂ = 0.25): Municipal lighting coverage.
  • Night Multiplier (w₃ = 0.25): Escalates 22:00–04:00 IST.
  • Surveillance Coverage (w₄ = 0.15): Distance to nearest CCTV/PCR van.
Explainable AI (XAI) Output
  • Transparent factor breakdown for every city polygon.
  • Citizens see exact percentages for Lighting, Crime, and CCTV.
  • Actionable travel advisories (e.g., 'Prefer WHC Road over internal lanes after 10 PM').
  • Calibrated across Sitabuldi, Dharampeth, Mahal, and Sadar.
Speaker Script: "N-SHIELD's algorithm recalculates risk dynamically based on the hour of the day and illumination levels. Furthermore, every score is completely explainable to the user."
SAFE ROUTING
Dynamic Incident Re-Routing Engine
Shortest vs. Safest Path
  • The Problem: Shortest route algorithms cut through dark alleys and isolated industrial stretches.
  • The N-SHIELD Solution: Scans a 750m buffer envelope around candidate paths.
  • Prioritizes well-lit, police-patrolled arterial avenues.
  • Visual color-coding: Green (Safe), Yellow (Caution), Red (High Risk).
Real-Time Incident Interception
  • Monitors live road disruptions, protests, and waterlogging.
  • Automatically reconstructs alternate paths bypassing incidents.
  • Provides turn-by-turn safety rationale directly to commuter.
  • One-tap export to Google Maps Navigation.
Speaker Script: "Our safe routing engine evaluates candidate paths using a 750-meter safety envelope, avoiding dark spots and dynamically recalculating around disruptions."

EMERGENCY TELEMETRY
Emergency Circle & One-Tap Live Sharing
One-Tap SOS Broadcast
  • Pre-configure parents, siblings, and trusted guardians.
  • Single tap sends emergency SMS/WhatsApp with live tracking link.
  • Instantly connects to Central Police (112) or Ambulance (108).
  • Begins high-frequency GPS coordinate broadcast.
Zero-Install Recipient View
  • Guardians DO NOT need to install an app or create an account.
  • Streams live moving marker, speed, heading, and battery %.

  • Ephemeral 32-byte cryptographic tokens with auto-expiry.
